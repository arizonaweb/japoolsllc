# Pool Service Request Form - Quick Summary

## ✅ What's New

### 1. **Auto-Populated Address** 🗺️
- Geolocation automatically detects your current location
- Instantly fills in your pool address during Step 4
- Validates you're in Phoenix service area
- You can still manually edit the address anytime
- Shows "Auto-filled" badge when complete

### 2. **Images Automatically Stored & Ready for Submission** 📸
- All 3 photos you capture are automatically saved to your device
- **No upload delays** - photos stay on your device until you choose how to send them
- Choose your preferred submission method on the final step:
  - 📧 **Email**: Opens your email client (you add photos manually)
  - 💬 **Text/SMS**: Opens your messaging app
  - 📋 **Copy**: Copies your request to clipboard for any app
  - ⬇️ **Download**: Download all photos to your device
- Photos stored safely in your browser for 24 hours

### 3. **Effortless Fast Flow** ⚡
- 9-step wizard (takes ~3-5 minutes with photos)
- Live progress bar shows where you are
- One question per step (not overwhelming)
- Photos display in final review before choosing submission method
- Multiple ways to submit - pick what works for you

### 4. **Clear Photo Instructions** 📷
- Step 1: Take photo of entire pool (stand back far)
- Step 2: Take photo of spa or pool details/concerns
- Step 3: Take photo of equipment pad (pump, filter, valves)
- Each photo is labeled and confirmed before moving on
- Option to retake any photo at any time

### 5. **Flexible Submission Methods** ✨
- **Step 8**: Review complete request with all info + photos
- **Step 9**: Choose your submission method
  - Email, SMS/Text, Copy to Clipboard, or Download Photos
- No account needed - works with your existing apps
- You can switch methods if you change your mind

## 🔧 Technical Features

**Address Auto-Population:**
- Uses browser geolocation (requires permission + HTTPS)
- OpenStreetMap reverse geocoding for accuracy
- Validates against 150+ Phoenix/Valley ZIP codes
- Graceful fallback if geolocation unavailable

**Photo Storage:**
- IndexedDB browser storage (no external server)
- Automatic quality optimization (0.82 JPEG quality)
- Scaled to 1600px max width for efficiency
- All 3 photos stored securely on your device
- Automatic cleanup after 24 hours

**Flexible Submission:**
- **Email**: Opens default email client with request
- **SMS/MMS**: Opens messaging app with request
- **Copy**: Clipboard API with fallback for all browsers
- **Download**: Direct download of all photos as JPEG files

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need WiFi to use the camera?**  
A: No, camera works on cellular data. You only need internet when submitting.

**Q: What if geolocation doesn't work?**  
A: You can manually type your address - it's still optional, just convenient.

**Q: Can I retake a photo?**  
A: Yes! Each photo can be retaken anytime until you submit.

**Q: Will the photos be saved somewhere permanently?**  
A: No, photos stay on YOUR device. They're only sent when you choose to submit, and they automatically delete after 24 hours.

**Q: What if I close the form?**  
A: All data is cleared when you close the form. You'll need to start over.

**Q: Does it work on my phone?**  
A: Yes! Works on iPhone and Android (needs a browser with camera support).

**Q: What if I forget something?**  
A: The form won't let you proceed until all required fields are filled.

**Q: How do I know it was received?**  
A: You'll get a call, text, AND email within 1 business day confirming your appointment.

**Q: Can I submit without photos?**  
A: No, all 3 photos are required for accurate estimates and fast service.

**Q: What's the "Copy" option?**  
A: It copies your entire request text to your clipboard so you can paste it into WhatsApp, email, or any other app.

**Q: Can I download the photos separately?**  
A: Yes! Step 9 has a "Download Photos" option to save all images to your device.

**Q: What if the email/SMS doesn't work?**  
A: You can use the Copy option to paste into any messaging app, or manually attach the downloaded photos to an email.
